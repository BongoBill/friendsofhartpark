Silents is Back, this time with a Halloween theme!

A Haunted Night of Heritage

Dare to step into the shadowy grounds of William S. Hart Park as Silents Under the Stars takes a chilling turn for autumn! As dusk falls over Hart Hall, gather with us in the hall for a thrilling cinematic night featuring the 1925 silent horror classic The Phantom of the Opera starring Lon Chaney. Accompanied by a spellbinding live score by Ray Lowe, your terrifyingly fun evening also includes a delicious dinner and dessert, a silent auction, and musical surprises hidden in the dark!
